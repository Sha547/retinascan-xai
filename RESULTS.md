## Model and protocol

- Model `effnet-b0-512-exp1`: efficientnet_b0 (ImageNet-pretrained, fine-tuned), 512 px, retina crop + CLAHE; checkpoint from epoch 11, chosen by validation QWK (0.906).
- Split: image-level stratified group split 70/15/15 (near-duplicates grouped); APTOS has no patient IDs, so patient-level separation is not possible.
- Fitted on validation only: temperature, referable_threshold, abstain_threshold, model selection.
- Test split: final scoring only, once. No test-time augmentation.
- Temperature T = 2.040; referable if P(grade >= 2) >= 0.608 (chosen for validation sensitivity >= 0.9); abstain if max probability < 0.532 (chosen for about 85.0% validation coverage).

## Where Grad-CAM points (test split, n = 546)

Rim = the outer 15% of the retina radius. If the heatmap were spread evenly, the rim's share of heatmap weight would equal its share of the area (ratio 1.0). Higher means the model leans on the image border.

Peripheral lesions are common in advanced DR, so a higher rim share for severe and proliferative cases is not by itself evidence of a shortcut, but it is also not evidence against one. Treat the heatmap as a coarse hint.

## Limitations (read before quoting any number)

- **Image-level split.** APTOS has no patient IDs, so two photos of the same patient (e.g. both eyes) can fall in different splits. Near-duplicate images are grouped, but this does not remove same-patient leakage; test numbers may be optimistic.
- **Not real PHC images.** Per the Kaggle data description, APTOS images were gathered from multiple clinics using a variety of cameras. Images from rural primary health centres may differ and be worse. See the external and degradation sections.
- **Label noise.** APTOS grades come from single clinicians; conflicting duplicate labels were found (see Data).
- **Operating point does not transfer.** The referable threshold fixed on APTOS validation gives sensitivity 0.724 on IDRiD vs 0.924 on APTOS test, while AUC stays 0.938. A new site would need its own threshold, set on its own labelled validation data, before use.
- **Weak classes.** Mild and severe NPDR are the least accurate grades (see the confusion matrix). The referable decision is more reliable than the exact grade.
- **Grad-CAM is coarse.** It shows regions that influenced the grade, at about 32 px resolution. It is not lesion detection or segmentation.
- **Calibration** is fitted on one validation split of about 550 images; ECE is noisy at this size.
- **Not clinically validated.** No prospective study, no clinician-reviewed pilot, no regulatory review.
