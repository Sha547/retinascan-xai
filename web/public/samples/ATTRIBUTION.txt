Sample photos in this folder are from the Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD),
official disease-grading TEST set, resized to 1024 px. Licence: CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: resized and re-encoded as JPEG;
idrid_test_IDRiD_030_degraded.jpg is additionally darkened and blurred on purpose.

Porwal P, Pachade S, Kokare M, et al. Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD).
IEEE DataPort, 2018. doi:10.21227/H25W98
Porwal P, et al. Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD): A Database for Diabetic
Retinopathy Screening Research. Data 3(3):25, 2018. doi:10.3390/data3030025
